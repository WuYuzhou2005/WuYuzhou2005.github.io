Research

Insurance, incentives, and risk

Insurance contract design

Optimal insurance contracts with claim-triggered auditing under moral hazard

I study how insurance coverage and claim-triggered auditing can be designed jointly when policyholders’ preventive effort is unobservable. The work examines incentive compatibility and optimal contracts under distortion risk measures, including Value-at-Risk and Conditional Value-at-Risk.

Auditing and incentives

Loss-dependent auditing and insurance contract design

This research explores insurance contracts with audit probabilities that depend on reported losses. I investigate how auditing rules interact with coverage and policyholder incentives, including the role of threshold rules and randomized auditing.

Projects

Machine learning reliability

Language models · Calibration

Confidence calibration in base and instruction-tuned language models

I compare base and instruction-tuned models on multiple-choice questions to examine how their confidence relates to answer correctness. The project studies confidence calibration, temperature scaling, answer-label bias, and consistency under permutations of answer choices.

A central question is whether permutation consistency provides useful information about correctness beyond model confidence.

  • Confidence calibration
  • Temperature scaling
  • Answer-label bias
  • Permutation consistency
