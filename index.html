Yuzhou Wu

Undergraduate in Actuarial Science

I study insurance, incentives, and decisions under uncertainty.

I am a BSc student in Actuarial Science at the University of Hong Kong, with expected graduation in June 2027. I am currently an exchange student at the University of Toronto.

My research interests include actuarial science, insurance economics, optimization under uncertainty, risk and decision analysis, and quantitative finance.

I collaborate with Prof. Fangda Liu at the University of Waterloo on loss-dependent auditing and insurance design under moral hazard. My work with Prof. Tim J. Boonen at HKU examines optimal insurance contracts under distortion risk measures.
