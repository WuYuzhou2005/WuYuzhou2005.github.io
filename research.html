Current Research

Loss-dependent auditing and insurance design

With Prof. Fangda Liu · University of Waterloo · July 2026–present

We are developing an insurance model with loss-dependent auditing, imperfect effort verification, and strategic claim reporting under moral hazard. We study the joint design of audit frequency, verification quality, and insurance coverage under incentive compatibility and verification-cost constraints.
